Quantum-Enhanced Bayesian Risk Estimation

Combining Bayesian financial risk modelling with quantum amplitude estimation.

RStanPythonQiskitBayesian inferenceMCMCQuantum computing

Objective

I developed this project independently during the Junction 2026 hackathon as part of the OP Quantum Finance Challenge. The objective was to explore the potential of quantum amplitude estimation in Bayesian financial risk assessment and compare it with classical probability estimation.

Key findings

Bayesian risk modelling produced a 95% VaR of −2.33% and a CVaR of −2.93%.

Classical and quantum probability estimates were nearly identical, with a relative difference of only 0.0426%.

Although quantum amplitude estimation has a theoretical advantage in computational complexity, the results did not demonstrate a practical efficiency advantage over classical Monte Carlo.
